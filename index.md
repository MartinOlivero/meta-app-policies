# iamautom — Legal & Platform Policies

Policies for the applications operated by **Martín Olivero** (iamautom) on the Meta and
TikTok platforms. Each application has its own policy: the applications serve different
purposes and process different data.

**Operator:** Martín Olivero — iamautom
**Contact:** [contacto@iamautom.com](mailto:contacto@iamautom.com)
**Website:** [iamautom.com](https://iamautom.com)

---

## AutomLead

Lead-management platform for car dealerships. Buyers contact a dealership over
WhatsApp; the platform qualifies the enquiry, routes it to a salesperson and gives
the dealership visibility over response times.

Processes personal data of **third parties** (people who message a dealership) on
behalf of the dealership.

- [Privacy Policy](/meta-app-policies/automlead/privacy-policy/)
- [Data Deletion Instructions](/meta-app-policies/automlead/privacy-policy/#data-deletion)
- [Terms of Service](/meta-app-policies/automlead/terms-of-service/)

## Tincho_I_G

First-party publishing and engagement tool for the Instagram account
**@tincho.olivero** and the Facebook Page **IamAutom**, both owned by the operator.
Publishes content, reads engagement on that content, and sends automated private
replies to people who comment asking for a resource.

- [Privacy Policy](/meta-app-policies/tincho-ig/privacy-policy/)
- [Data Deletion Instructions](/meta-app-policies/tincho-ig/privacy-policy/#data-deletion)

## iamautom (TikTok)

First-party content publishing tool for the operator's own TikTok account. Uploads the
operator's own videos, reads the operator's own profile and creator settings. Does not
read or process data of any other TikTok user.
